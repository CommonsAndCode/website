The digital and physical worlds are inextricably intertwined.
Policy for this new reality must be more than the mere digitalisation of outdated processes.
It must actively shape people’s everyday lives.

As a network of technology experts, local government practitioners and engaged citizens, we build bridges between technology and politics.
We turn European visions into concrete, local solutions that strengthen democratic participation and enable sustainable transformation.

**Democratically, Sustainably, and in the European Spirit.**
