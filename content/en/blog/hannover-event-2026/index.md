Do you want to help your community with digitalisation, for example in your new role as a city council member?
Then join our introductory workshop **on 26 September in Hanover**!

**When?** 14:00 - 16:30, 26 September 2026<br>
**Where?** Stadtteilzentrum Stöcken, Eichsfelder Straße 101, 30419 Hanover

We offer you an introduction to the most important topics of local digital policy.
How bad is it really?
What proven practices exist?
How do you go from proposal to implementation?

You bring curiosity and your own ideas.
We bring experience from municipal politics and IT.
Together we start your successful digital policy.

Afterwards, you are cordially invited to socialise with us.
We look forward to the exchange!
